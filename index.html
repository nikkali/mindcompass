MindCompass

Build a repeatable workflow to discover, vet, score, and follow Solana scalp wallets.

1. Configure live data (optional)

MindCompass works offline, but you can paste API keys to pull real trade activity directly from Birdeye and Solscan when reviewing a wallet.

2. Hunt & seed your pipeline

Capture candidate wallets you spot in Birdeye’s Find Trades feed. Use the quick checklist to mark what you noticed before you promote them.

Discovery checklist

  • Seen ≥2 trades in the last hour from Birdeye Find Trades feed.
  • Ticket size between $1K–$10K or >$10K (avoid dust and outliers).
  • Repeats liquid pairs (not taxed or honeypot tokens).
  • Venues look consistent (Jupiter / Orca / Raydium rather than unknown routers).
  • Grab Solscan link and add to Watchlist for monitoring.

3. Verify, score & promote

Promote wallets scoring ≥7/10.

Watch (candidates)

New finds. Track for a session or two before promoting.

Follow (active scalp wallets)

Proven wallets with alerts enabled and scoring ≥7.
